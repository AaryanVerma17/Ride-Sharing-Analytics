Where does the data come from?

Synthetic ride-sharing data representing riders, drivers and trips across cities and time. The dataset is designed to create realistic operational patterns while remaining safe for demonstration.

How is revenue counted?

Revenue comes from completed trips. Cancelled trips remain visible as lost-fare opportunities rather than being counted as realised revenue.

What powers the dashboard?

MySQL stores the analytical model, Flask exposes parameterised API queries, and Plotly renders the interactive analytical views in the browser.
